RateHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RateHub ("the app") is a currency converter developed by Papet. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we do not collect

RateHub does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment details. You do not need an account. The app does not show ads and does not collect the device advertising ID.

Information stored only on your device

Your exact amounts, conversion history, chosen currencies, theme and other settings are stored on your device. You can clear conversion history in Settings, and all of it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Information sent to Google Firebase

The app uses Google Firebase services:

  • Usage statistics (optional): Firebase Analytics and Performance Monitoring. Which screens, buttons, currencies and settings are used; rough amount ranges (for example "100–1k", never the exact amount); app performance; device model, OS and app version; approximate country derived from the IP address; and an app instance ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this is off until you allow it in the app. Everywhere you can turn it off any time in Settings › Privacy › Share usage statistics.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics. Crash logs and diagnostic data, so we can fix bugs.
  • Anonymous sign-in: Firebase Authentication. A random identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging. A device token used to deliver notifications and in-app messages. On Android 13 and later notifications are only shown if you allow them.
  • App settings: Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations. An installation ID used to fetch app configuration.
  • App integrity: Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity). Checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

Exchange rates and market prices are downloaded from our own servers. These requests do not include any personal information.

How we use information and legal bases

We use this information to provide exchange rates, keep the app secure, fix crashes, measure performance, understand which features are used, and send notifications. Usage statistics are processed with your consent where the law requires it, and otherwise on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the app. Crash reports, anonymous sign-in, notifications and app-integrity checks are processed to provide the service and keep it working. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google as our service provider under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Google may process data outside your country. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash data for up to 90 days. The anonymous identifier is kept until you uninstall the app.

Your rights and choices

  • Turn usage statistics off or on in Settings › Privacy.
  • Turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to stop all collection.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you can ask us to access, correct or delete data linked to your installation, object to processing, or complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address below.

Delete your data

To delete the data RateHub has collected about your installation:

  1. Open RateHub › Settings › Privacy and turn off Share usage statistics to stop further analytics.
  2. Email dev.mobinjafari@gmail.com with the subject "RateHub data deletion". We will reply with simple steps to identify your installation.
  3. We delete the analytics, crash and anonymous-account data linked to your installation within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data deleted: usage statistics, crash reports, the anonymous account ID and the notification token. Nothing is kept after deletion, except aggregated statistics that no longer identify your installation. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on your device.

Children

RateHub is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
