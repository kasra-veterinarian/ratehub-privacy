RateHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

RateHub ("the app") is a currency converter developed by Papet. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we do not collect

RateHub does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. You do not need an account to use the app.

Information collected automatically

The app uses Google Firebase services to keep the app working and to improve it:

  • Firebase Authentication (anonymous): creates a random anonymous identifier for your installation. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Firebase Analytics: collects how the app is used (for example, screens opened, buttons tapped, currencies selected), device model, OS version, app version, approximate region derived from IP, and the device advertising ID / app instance ID.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring: collects crash reports, diagnostic and performance data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: uses a device token to deliver notifications if you allow them.
  • Firebase Remote Config: fetches app settings.

The amounts you type and your conversion history are stored only on your device.

How we use information

To provide exchange rates, fix crashes, measure performance, understand which features are used, and send optional notifications. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. The app does not show ads.

Sharing

Data is processed by Google as our service provider under the Firebase privacy terms. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash data for up to 90 days.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in system settings, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or uninstall the app to stop all collection. To ask for deletion of data linked to your installation, contact us at the address below.

Children

RateHub is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.
